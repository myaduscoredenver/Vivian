About the neighborhood

On the west side of Old Town Longmont, Vivian’s tree-lined streets have a mix of early bungalows and cottages, midcentury ranches, and a few newer homes. Original hardwood floors, front porches, and built-ins are recurring details. Outside, listings often feature fenced backyards, garden beds, and detached garages, sometimes with alley access. Sunset Park, Sunset Pool, and Sunset Golf Course are nearby.
